## Voice

<!-- Describe how your brand sounds. 2-3 sentences. -->
We sound like a knowledgeable friend, not a salesperson. Warm but specific. We explain why something works without dumbing it down.

## Tone by context

<!-- How does the tone shift for different content types? -->
- **Landing pages**: confident, specific, grounded. Lead with what the customer gets, not how we feel about it.
- **Emails**: conversational, personal. First-person from the founder when appropriate.
- **Social**: casual, visual-first. Short sentences.
